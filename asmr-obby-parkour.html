Privacy policy

This policy describes how ZZ GameZ handles data in ASMR Obby Parkour for iOS.

Guest accounts and gameplay

Online features use a Unity guest account. Unity assigns a player ID. Your nickname, scores, parkour completion times, recorded runs, and friend invitations are processed to run rankings, ghost races, and friend races. Your nickname and game results may be visible to other players in those features. Some progress and settings are stored on your device.

Unity processes online game data through its authentication, cloud storage, and multiplayer services. See the Unity Privacy Policy.

Advertising and analytics

The game uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, app interactions, ad activity, and diagnostic data to provide and measure ads and prevent fraud. Ad choices depend on your location and consent preferences. Where applicable, use Privacy Options in Settings to review or change your ad consent choices.

Where enabled, Firebase Analytics measures activity such as level starts and completions, screen views, and in-game currency use. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Purchases and support

Apple processes payments made through the App Store. The game uses purchase status to provide purchased features. If you email us for support, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond.

Account deletion and privacy requests

In the game, open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. This deletes your guest account, online progress and scores, and local game progress. Manage any active App Store subscription separately in your Apple account settings.

For questions about your data or help exercising your privacy rights, email zzgamez@gmail.com.
