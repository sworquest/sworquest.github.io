Privacy policy

This policy covers Car Parkour Race 2 on iPhone and iPad. Updated 21 November 2026.

You can start playing as soon as you open the game. Settings, course progress and vehicle unlocks are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes its local game data.

The app requests non-personalized ads through Google AdMob. The game offers optional rewarded videos and may display an interstitial at a course retry or respawn. Google may process approximate location inferred from your connection, device identifiers, ad and interaction information, and crash and performance data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate the service. See how Google uses data from apps.

In regions where ad consent choices are required, open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or update them.

We use the email address and information you share when contacting us to respond to your request. For privacy questions, contact zzgamez@gmail.com.

GitHub’s privacy statement describes how GitHub Pages may process technical connection data when you visit this website.
